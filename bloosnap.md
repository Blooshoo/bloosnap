Core Extension Ideas

    Automated Scrolling Capture: When you click the icon (or press Alt + Shift + P), the extension scrolls down the entire webpage, takes rapid snapshots, and stitches them together into a single, high-resolution image.

    Handles Complex Elements: It handles inner scrollable sections, embedded iframes, and sticky headers/bars that normally break standard scrolling screenshot tools.

    Smart Splitting: If a page is too massive for Chrome's canvas limits to hold in a single file, it splits the capture cleanly across separate tabs.

    Multiple Export Formats: You can export the resulting image directly to PNG, JPG, or standard PDF paper sizes, or drag-and-drop the finished image right out of the tab.

    Privacy & Lightweight: It requires minimal permissions (activeTab), 


    Annotation Tools: Add text boxes, shapes, arrows, highlights, and emojis directly onto the captured page.

    Redaction / Blurring: Blur or black out sensitive information, personal details, or credentials before saving or sharing.

    Image Cropping: Trim headers, footers, or margins directly in the extension without exporting to third-party software like GIMP or Photoshop.

    Metadata Watermarking: Automatically embed the source URL, capture date, and timestamps directly onto the image or exported PDF for auditing/archiving.

    Smart PDF Page Splitting: Adjusts PDF page breaks dynamically so lines of text or charts aren't sliced in half across pages.
